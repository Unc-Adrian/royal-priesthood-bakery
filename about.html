How it began

Birthed from 1 Peter 2:9, Royal Priesthood Bakery was born after a long search.

I belive a bakery is one of the most important institutions in any society, because every month holds a reason for joy.

And we are here to bake that joy for you!

Come in, take your time, and taste the difference that care makes.
